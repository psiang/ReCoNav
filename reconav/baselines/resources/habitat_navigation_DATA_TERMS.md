# Habitat navigation resources

This resource contains navmeshes derived from the nine HM3D/HM3DSem scenes, with a robot radius of 0.25 m and height of 1.5 m. It includes Habitat-Sim 0.2.1, 0.2.4 and 0.3.3 variants, construction metadata and exact-start availability records.
